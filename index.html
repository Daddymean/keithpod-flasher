Flash Keithpod

Install Keithpod on the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.06 watch. Use desktop Chrome or Edge (Web Serial). A USB-C data cable is required.

  1. Plug the watch in with USB-C.
  2. Click Connect, pick the watch's port, then Install.
  3. If no port appears, hold BOOT, tap PWR, release BOOT, then Connect again.
  4. Updating a watch that is already set up: choose not to erase. Nothing else to do.
  5. New watch: Wi-Fi is optional now (the phone app uses Bluetooth). Close the Wi-Fi dialog if Chrome shows one. The watch then shows a setup screen with its device secret: write it down (or join the Keithpod-setup Wi-Fi, open http://192.168.4.1 and copy it), then hold BOOT for 2 seconds to skip Wi-Fi.
  6. Pair the watch in the Keithpod Companion Android app: tap Pair watch and type the 6-digit code the watch shows. To pair a different phone later, double-tap BOOT on the 5-second boot screen.

First flash: erasing is fine. Do not erase when updating, or the watch forgets the device secret and its paired phone, and clips already on the card or the phone can no longer be decrypted.

Firmware v0.5.0 sends clips to your phone over Bluetooth LE (encrypted end to end with the device secret; the watch deletes a clip only after the phone confirms its checksum) and gets its clock from the phone, so the watch's Wi-Fi stays off. A FAT32 microSD card (32 GB or smaller is simplest) holds clips while the phone is away. The screen turns off after 8 seconds (raise your wrist, touch it, or tap PWR to wake it). Serial logs are at 115200 baud if anything looks wrong.

This page is static, costs nothing, and stores no secrets. Firmware source and longer notes live with the keithpod firmware tree (FIRMWARE.md).
